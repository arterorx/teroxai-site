App: AI Video Create: Text to Video ("AI Video Maker", the "App")

Effective date: July 3, 2026

Developer: Armen Ter-Oganezov ("we", "us", "our")

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the App.

**Account & identifiers**

An anonymous account is created automatically so the App can work without registration. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive a private Apple user identifier and, if you allow it, your email (which may be Apple's private relay address). We do not receive your Apple password.

A device identifier (Apple IDFV — Identifier for Vendor) used to prevent abuse and fraud (e.g., ban evasion).

A push notification token if you enable notifications.

**Content you provide**

The text prompts you enter to generate videos. Prompts are sent to our AI processing providers to create the video and to automated moderation services to detect prohibited content.

Generated videos are stored on your device. When you tap Save, they are written to your photo library. We do not host a public feed of your content.

**Purchases**

Subscription and coin-pack purchase events (product purchased, transaction status). Payments are processed by Apple; we do not receive your card or payment details.

**Usage, diagnostics & advertising data**

App usage and interaction events, crash and performance data (analytics).

If you consent, an advertising identifier (IDFA) and related data used to serve and measure ads.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, health data, your microphone, or your camera, and we do not read your photo library.

## 2. How we use your information

- To provide the core feature — generating videos from your prompts.
- To manage your coins, subscription status, streaks, and referral rewards.
- To operate content moderation and prevent abuse, fraud, and prohibited content.
- To send push notifications you opted into (e.g., streak and reward reminders).
- To process purchases and restore them.
- To display advertising (to non-subscribers) and measure its performance.
- To analyze usage, fix bugs, and improve the App.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Third-party services

We use the following providers, who process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

- Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Cloud Messaging, App Check) — accounts, app data, analytics, notifications, anti-abuse.
- fal.ai — AI video generation from your prompts.
- OpenAI — automated moderation of prompts.
- RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management.
- Google AdMob (with the Google User Messaging Platform) — advertising and consent management.
- Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store payments.

## 4. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

- with the service providers listed above, to operate the App;
- with advertising partners, only where you have consented (see Advertising below);
- when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of users and the public.

## 5. Advertising and tracking

Non-subscribers may see ads served by Google AdMob.

On first launch we ask, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency, for permission before any tracking that uses your advertising identifier. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using tracking.

In the EEA and the UK, we present a consent form (Google UMP) before personalized ads are shown.

You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## 6. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists and as needed for the purposes above.

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Profile → Delete account). This permanently removes your server-side account data, coins, streak, referral, and subscription record on our side. Purchases already made are non-refundable and are governed by Apple's terms; you may still need to cancel an active subscription in your Apple account settings.

Some data may be retained where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR in the EEA/UK, CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, use in-app account deletion or contact us at the email below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Data security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), server-authoritative access controls, and app attestation, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

## 10. International data transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new effective date, and material changes will be reflected in the App.
