This site is a static website run by Armen Ter-Oganezov, Germany.

## What is collected

Nothing that identifies you. The site sets no cookies and has no forms, accounts or comments.

Traffic is measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts page views without cookies and without storing your IP address or fingerprinting your browser. Cloudflare's description of what it does is at cloudflare.com/web-analytics.

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the page and to protect against abuse. Cloudflare's privacy policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

## Links to other sites

Links to the App Store, GitHub, LinkedIn and the individual app websites lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

## Email

If you write to hello@teroxai.com, your message and address are kept as long as needed to answer you, and then as long as ordinary email retention requires. They are not shared with anyone.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data exists about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to a data protection authority. Since this site stores nothing about you, in practice this applies only to email. Write to hello@teroxai.com.

## Apps

Each app has its own policy. They are linked from the app's page on this site.
